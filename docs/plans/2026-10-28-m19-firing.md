## 1. Goal and scope

- **Goal:** pressing "Invia" sends a course to the kitchen and is the one moment stock moves: each plate takes its portions FEFO and its raw ingredients, its cost is frozen out of the waiter's sight, and the kitchen sees a live ticket list without prices. Voids are reason-coded and never delete anything.
- **In scope:**
  - `public.fire_course(p_session, {lineIds})`: single transaction, idempotent on replay, FEFO batch depletion (0,5 supported, across batches), raw-ingredient depletion through the M12 engine, frozen cost in `order_line_cost`, shortages booked and alerted, never refused for stock.
  - `public.void_line(p_line, {reason, clientUpdatedAt})`: before firing no movement; after firing reversal + waste per consumption row. Coded reasons. Re-fire = void + new line + fire.
  - `public.kitchen_tickets()` and `GET /api/kitchen/tickets` (chef, owner, admin; no €), polling, 60 s stale banner, high-contrast kitchen mode, screen `/cucina` (phone and laptop).
  - Waiter screen `/tavoli/[sessionId]`: "Invia" per course and "Invia a seguire" per course; "Storna" with a reason on sent lines.
  - `app.session_fired_lines` replaced (table status "courses sent", M17 stub); the nightly ledger check gains `fire_depletion` (project §3.6 rule 7).
  - The `negative_stock` alert template accepts the batch shortage payload; the alert links to "Produzione".
  - pgTAP `170`–`179`, ten race cases, Vitest, an integration suite, Playwright on `phone`, `phone-webkit` and a new `laptop` project.
- **Out of scope:**
  - offline queue, `applied_op`, `/api/sync` (M20; §3.4 gives the contract);
  - bill, close, `app.plates_sold` (M21/M22: `plates_sold` stays the M8 stub);
  - ticket "done"/bump, printer, KDS (M40);
  - a confirmation step before sending (Q102 → V6); chef-side sending (Q99 → V3); instant kitchen updates (Q98 → V2);
  - editing the price or the dish of a sent line (a void + new line);
  - waste € reports for voided plates (M22 reads `order_line_cost` + the void rows).

## 3. Design

### 3.1 Decisions

1. **What "Invia" fires (PRD 3.3; Q97 → V1).** The client sends the ids of the lines it shows; the server never computes "all drafts of the course", because a replay of "fire primi" would then also fire primi added later (not idempotent).
   - The sheet shows per course **"Invia"** (draft lines with `segue` false) and **"Invia a seguire"** (draft lines with `segue` true). Segue dishes stay drafts until the waiter calls them; firing them is the depletion moment (PRD 2.3).
   - The server accepts any 1–50 distinct lines of **one session**, of any courses. Tickets are grouped afterwards (decision 8).
2. **`fire_course` semantics and idempotency.**
   - Lines already fired → `skipped {reason: already_fired}`; voided → `skipped {reason: voided}`; nothing is raised. A replay therefore writes nothing and answers 200 with the lines as they are (M20 AT3/AT9 contract).
   - One instant `v_now` (`clock_timestamp()` once) for every line of the call: `fired_at = v_now`, `fired_by` = caller. `updated_*` and `client_updated_at` are **not** touched (the guard requires `client_updated_at` unchanged on fired lines, and "who edited last" stays true).
   - The fire is not subject to last-write-wins: firing is irreversible and wins over any edit (ADR-017 rule 1). It fires the line **as it is when locked** (an edit committed a moment earlier is what the kitchen cooks; race `fire-vs-edit`).
   - Shared plates follow M18: `is_shared` is a flag on one plate and depletes as its `qty` (project §5a M18). Half portions: `qty` 0,5 depletes 0,5 × need.
3. **Batch depletion: FEFO with recorded shortage (PRD 2.2; ADR-002; §3.6 rule 8).** Per fired line `L` with a mapped plate, per batch key `K` of `app.plate_batch_needs` (current version), the requirement is `r = round(L.qty × need, 4)` portions.
   - **Candidates** (after the row locks, in a new statement): batches of `K` with `state in ('fresh','open')` and `use_by > v_now`, ordered by `use_by`, `produced_at`, `id` (FEFO; the same "live" as M18's availability, so "Ne restano N" and depletion agree; Q101 → V5).
   - Take `min(r_left, app.batch_remaining(b))` from each in order: one `consume` movement per batch slice, `portions = −slice`, `ref_type 'order_line'`, `ref_id = L.id`, `effective_on = app.venue_date(v_now, tz)`. Then `app.batch_settle_state(tenant, b, 'consume')`.
   - **Shortage** (`r_left > 0` after every live batch): the remainder is booked on the **target**:
     - the last live batch drained, if any live batch existed;
     - else the **fallback**: the most recently produced batch of `K` that is not `discarded`, else the most recently produced one (`produced_at desc, id desc`);
     - else nothing is booked (`K` has never had a batch, e.g. the plate got a new version with a new batch after the line was added); `K` goes to `unbooked_batch_keys` of the cost row.
   - The target goes below zero and settles `finished` (M15's state rule and the `batch_state` nightly check both expect `remaining ≤ 0 ⇔ finished/discarded`; `137` already accepts "a finished batch below 0").
   - **Negative balances are allowed** (decision, with ADR-002 and §3.6 rule 8): the food was served, refusing would lose the sale and the depletion record; M18 still refuses *entry* at 0. The chef corrects with M15 `adjust_batch` (a count reopens a finished batch).
   - **Alert** (contained block, `app.try_raise_alert`, the M12/M16 alert path; dedup = one per batch key per Rome business day): kind `negative_stock`, `entity_type 'recipe_key'`, `entity_id = K`, payload `{recipe_key, batch_id (target or null), remaining_portions_qty (target's balance after, or 0), short_portions_qty, order_line_id, movement_id (or null)}`. Every key passes `app.chef_payload_allowed`; no new alert kind.
4. **Raw-ingredient depletion ("direct raw ingredients", PRD 2.3; AT3).**
   - **Needs per plate:** new `app.plate_raw_needs(tenant, recipe_id)`: the plate version's top-level ingredient lines (`qty × recipe_unit_factor(unit) / yield_factor`, base units) plus the ingredient lines of its `sub` recipes, expanded with the sub version in force, scaled by `(line qty × factor / yield) / sub.output_qty`, depth guard 12 (the algorithm of `app.batch_requirements`). **Batch-type sub keys are skipped** (they are portions, decision 3). Same ingredient on two paths is summed.
   - Per stock ingredient (`is_stock`), `q = round(L.qty × need, 4)`, skipped when 0. Non-stock ingredients are costed (decision 6), not depleted.
5. **Which location raw stock comes from (Q100 → V4).** Pure FEFO **across** active locations, through the unchanged M12 engine:
   - the positive **lotted** buckets of the ingredient in every location, ordered by lot expiry (nulls last), `received_on`, lot id; for each, `app.post_stock_outflow(…, location, min(left, bucket), 'consumption', null, lot, 'order_line', L.id, v_now, v_now, 'fire_course')` (the lot override takes exactly that slice);
   - then positive **unlotted** buckets, by location id (`p_lot` null; by then every lot is empty, so the engine reaches the unlotted bucket);
   - a remainder → one call with `p_lot` null at the **shortfall location**: the location of the last bucket used, else that of the ingredient's most recent movement, else the lowest-id active location. The engine writes the unlotted negative row and raises its own contained `negative_stock` (entity `ingredient`).
   - No active location at all → no raw movement; the ingredient is listed in `missing_ingredient_ids` (cost unknown, never 0).
   - With one location (the venue's stated set-up, project §1.5) this is exactly M12's FEFO.
6. **Cost frozen at firing: `order_line_cost` (project §3.5.2; AT7).** One row per fired line, written in the fire transaction, append-only:
   - `recipe_id` = the plate version used (null when the menu item is unmapped);
   - `total_cost` = Σ over the line's consume movements `−portions × batch_cost.unit_cost_frozen` of that batch + Σ over its consumption stock rows `−qty × unit_cost` + Σ non-stock needs `L.qty × need × ingredient_unit_costs(v_now)`; rounded to 4 decimals;
   - `unit_cost_at_sale = round(total_cost / L.qty, 4)` (per full plate, like `unit_price`);
   - unknown is never zero: `incomplete` with `missing_batch_ids` (a batch whose cost is incomplete), `missing_ingredient_ids` (unpriced), `unbooked_batch_keys` (decision 3); an unmapped item is `incomplete` with empty lists; `total_cost` and `unit_cost_at_sale` are then null;
   - a later price, batch or recipe change never touches the row (append-only trigger).
   - **Who reads it:** owner and admin (RLS), `service_role` select-only (lesson 2). **Not the chef** — the brief and project §3.5.2 say chef, but founder Q16 = project V6 removed every chef cost read and `059` enforces it (Q104). Never the waiter (AT9).
7. **Voids (PRD 2.3; AT6).** `void_line(p_line, {reason, clientUpdatedAt})`; reasons are a coded list (`app.order_void_reason_codes()`; Q103), each mapped to an existing waste code that both `app.batch_waste_reason_codes()` and `app.stock_reason_codes()` contain:

   | Reason code | Italian label | Waste code (after firing) |
   |---|---|---|
   | `order_error` | Errore di comanda | `plating_error` |
   | `customer_changed` | Il cliente ha cambiato idea | `over_production` |
   | `kitchen_error` | Errore in cucina | `plating_error` |
   | `returned` | Rimandato indietro | `plating_error` |
   | `comp` | Offerto dalla casa | `comp` |
   | `breakage` | Caduto o rotto | `breakage` |

   - `entry_removed` stays M18's internal reason for "Rimuovi" and is **not** accepted by `void_line`.
   - **Before firing:** last-write-wins exactly as `remove_order_line` (stale/equal-stamp rules), then `voided_*` set and `client_updated_at`/`updated_*` moved; **no movement in either ledger** (stock unchanged).
   - **After firing:** the line's stamp is not compared (a fired line cannot have been edited since); the guard forbids changing `client_updated_at`, so only `voided_*` and `updated_*` move. For **each** of the line's consume movements: a reversal (`type consume`, `portions = −m.portions > 0`, `reverses_id = m.id`, `ref_type 'order_void'`, `ref_id = L.id`) **and** a waste row (`type waste`, `portions = m.portions`, mapped reason, `ref_type 'order_void'`, same batch). For **each** consumption stock row: a reversal mirroring lot, location, type and `unit_cost` (the M12 reversal guard checks it) and a waste row on the same lot and location with the mapped reason and the same `unit_cost`. Net effect per batch and per lot bucket: 0 — **stock is not restored**, and the plate moves from "consumed" to "waste" (M16's forecast counts reversals on the original date; M22's waste sees the waste rows). `order_line_cost` stays.
   - **Replay:** a voided line with the same reason by the same caller (and, for a draft void, the same stamp) → `{changed: false}`; any other → `conflict voided`. Closed session → `conflict session_closed`.
   - **Re-fire** (PRD 3.3) = void with reason + a new line (new `lineId`) + fire. No special procedure; the waiter re-adds the dish.
8. **Kitchen tickets.** A ticket = the lines fired in one call for one table, one course and one `segue` value: key `(table_session_id, fired_at, course, segue)`; React key `"<sessionId>|<firedAt>|<course>|<segue>"`.
   - Shown: lines with `fired_at` in the **current business day** (tenant cut-off, `app.business_date`) of sessions **not closed**; fired-then-voided lines stay, marked with their reason, so the kitchen stops cooking (Q105).
   - Order: `fired_at` ascending (oldest first, the kitchen's queue), then table name (`collate "C"`), course enum, `segue` false first; lines by `created_at`, id.
   - At most `KITCHEN_MAX_TICKETS` = 200 (the newest 200, returned ascending) with `truncated`.
   - **No € anywhere:** no `unitPrice`, cost, VAT or total; keys pass `059`'s chef regex.
   - `asOf` = the server instant of the read (shown as "Aggiornato alle 20:31").
9. **Refresh: polling (project §3.1/§3.8; Q98 → V2).** The project plan names Realtime with a 15 s polling fallback. M0 then made the session cookies HttpOnly with **no browser Supabase client**, so the browser has no token to open an authorised channel, and the chef has no RLS read of `order_line` anyway. Keeping it simple: the kitchen page reads `GET /api/kitchen/tickets` every `KITCHEN_POLL_MS` = 5 000 ms while visible, at once on `visibilitychange`/`focus`, with `latestOnly()`. One small RPC per 5 s per kitchen device. The waiter page keeps its 15 s refresh and refetches after its own fire/void.
10. **Stale banner (PRD NFR Offline; AT8).** `lastOkAt` = the client clock (`Date.now()`) at the last successful read; `isStale(lastOkAt, now) = now − lastOkAt > KITCHEN_STALE_MS` (60 000). Client clock only, so phone/server clock skew cannot trigger it. A 1 s ticker re-evaluates. Banner `role="alert"` in `main`: "Dati non aggiornati da più di un minuto. Controlla la connessione." The list stays visible underneath.
11. **High-contrast kitchen mode (PRD Accessibility).** A toggle "Alto contrasto" (`aria-pressed`) sets `data-contrast="high"` on the kitchen `main`; also on by default when `prefers-contrast: more`. The choice is a UI preference in `localStorage` (`kitchen.contrast`), not data. In that mode: no muted text (`ink-muted` → `ink`), 2 px `ink` borders, larger type for names and quantities; only token pairs ≥ 7:1 (WCAG AAA) from `src/lib/design/tokens.ts`, in light and dark.
12. **Who calls what (§3.11.2, project V6).**

    | Object / action | owner | admin | chef | waiter | DB enforcement | API |
    |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
    | `fire_course` | ✓ | ✓ | 42501 | ✓ | `app.require_table_operator()` (M17) | `POST /api/table-sessions/:id/fire` |
    | `void_line` | ✓ | ✓ | 42501 | ✓ | `app.require_table_operator()` | `POST /api/order-lines/:id/void` |

    - Every public function is `security definer`, `set search_path = ''`, checks the role **first**, takes the tenant from `app.tenant_id()` and the user from `app.app_user_id()`, filters every statement by tenant, and is executable by `authenticated` only. ### 3.3 Lock order (no deadlocks)

Global order, extending M12/M14/M15/M18 (each class in id order):

`table_session` FOR SHARE → `order_line` FOR UPDATE → ingredient stock advisory locks → `batch` rows FOR UPDATE / FOR SHARE → batch lot lock

| Procedure | Takes (in this order) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `add_order_line` | session SHARE → live batch rows SHARE | M18 |
| `update_order_line` | session SHARE → one line UPDATE → live batch rows SHARE (qty increase) | M18 |
| `remove_order_line` | session SHARE → one line UPDATE | M18 |
| **`fire_course`** | session SHARE → its lines UPDATE (id order) → stock locks (sorted) → batch targets UPDATE (id order) | M19 |
| **`void_line`** | session SHARE → one line UPDATE → stock locks → batch rows UPDATE | M19 |
| `produce_batch` | stock locks → lot lock (new batch row, no existing row lock) | M14 |
| `discard_batch`, `adjust_batch`, `split_batch` | one batch row UPDATE | M15 |
| `confirm_staff_meal` | rule row → batch rows **or** stock locks | M15 |
| `close_count` | count session → stock locks | M13 |
| `close_table` (future) | session UPDATE (waits for every SHARE above) | M21 contract |

- No procedure takes batch rows before stock locks, or lines after stock/batch locks, so no cycle exists. Two fires share no line set without one waiting on the lines first.
- FOR SHARE (adds) vs FOR UPDATE (fire, discard): an add waits for a fire's commit and then sees the reduced portions (M18 T2; race `add-vs-fire`).
- The engine re-takes stock locks it already holds (re-entrant, M12).
- **Accepted:** a batch produced while a fire waits is not among the locked targets and is used from the next fire (as M18's "seen on the next request").

### 3.7 Flows

1. **Send secondi:** tap → `POST /api/table-sessions/S/fire {lineIds:[L1,L2]}` → session SHARE → L1, L2 UPDATE → stock locks (polenta, pane) → stew batches A, B UPDATE → L1: A −1 (consume), polenta P2 −0,1 kg (Cella) + P1 −0,05 kg (Dispensa), pane −0,1 kg → cost row → L2 … → commit → 200. The kitchen's next 5 s read shows the ticket.
2. **Shortage:** stew live portions 0 (A, B finished): fire L3 → fallback B (−1 → B at −1, finished) → `negative_stock` alert for the stew key (owner + chef) → 200 as usual.
3. **Void after fire:** "Storna" → reason "Errore in cucina" → `POST /api/order-lines/L1/void` → reversal +1 and waste −1 (`plating_error`) on A; polenta and pane reversal + waste on the same lots → line voided → the kitchen card strikes the line on its next read. Re-fire: the waiter adds the dish again and sends.
4. **Two waiters send the same course:** the second request waits on the line locks, then answers `skipped already_fired` (race `fire-twice`).
5. **Kitchen offline:** reads fail → after 60 s without a success the banner shows; the next success hides it.

### 3.9 Tests convention

- pgTAP sets claims with `set_config('request.jwt.claims', …)`; fixtures no procedure can write (batches in each state with fixed `use_by`, lots, closed sessions, fault triggers) are inserted as the migration owner inside the rolled-back test; errors asserted as `SQLSTATE message`.
- **Rows, tickets and items are found by id, never by index** (`data-line-id`, `data-ticket-key`, `data-menu-item-id`; SQL by uuid).
- **Two of everything:** two locations, two stew batches plus expired/finished/discarded ones, two tenants, two sessions, two waiters, two courses in one fire, two raw ingredients.
- Integration and e2e create recipes, batches, lots and menu items through the APIs (`seedStewRecipe`, `approveWithLots`, M17/M18 helpers). The secret key creates staff and reads `order_line_cost`/`batch_movement`; `stock_movement` (no `service_role` grant) is read with `runSql` on the local DB (it asserts the URL is local). Never `recipe`/`recipe_key` with the secret key.
- Playwright:
  - selects: `getByRole("combobox", { name: /^Motivo\b/ })` (the label wraps the control);
  - alerts: `page.getByRole("main").getByRole("alert")` (the layout has other alert regions);
  - formatted numbers: regexes with `\s+` for U+00A0 (e.g. `/14,00\s+€/`, `/Ne restano\s+2/`) and U+2212 for minus signs;
  - "no €": scoped to the kitchen `main` (chef) and to the waiter's 403 element on `/cucina`; "no cost" on the waiter page = no "Costo", "Margine", "Food cost" (the waiter legitimately sees sale prices);
  - fake time with `page.clock` (`install`, `fastForward`) (T4).

## 4. Alternatives considered

- **Refuse firing on shortage:** rejected (ADR-002, §3.6 rule 8, PRD AT4): the plate was served.
- **Server picks "all drafts of the course":** rejected: a replayed fire would also send lines added later; the client's ids make replays exact.
- **A `kitchen_ticket` table with a client fire id:** rejected for M19 (ADR-019): a second write and a second idempotency key; tickets derive from `(session, fired_at, course, segue)`. M40 may add it.
- **Void after fire as a waste row only:** rejected: the plate would count as consumption and waste (M16 forecast, M22 reports). **Restore on void after fire:** rejected by PRD 2.3.
- **Map void reasons by widening the waste reason lists:** rejected: it touches `stock_reason_codes()` and two check constraints (M12, M15) and the TS lists pinned by `090`/`130`; a fixed mapping onto existing codes keeps them.
- **Supabase Realtime now (project §3.1):** rejected for the build: the browser holds no session token (M0's HttpOnly cookies) and the chef has no row-level read of `order_line`. Kept as V2.
- **Reuse `app.batch_requirements` for raw needs:** rejected: it expands batch keys into their raw ingredients, which were consumed at production (PRD).
- **Per-location raw depletion with `resolve_location`:** rejected: with two locations it raises `location_required`, and a waiter cannot choose. A fixed location is V4.

## 6. Risks and mitigations

- **Security / privacy**
  - *Definer functions bypass RLS:* role check first, empty `search_path`, tenant from `app.tenant_id()`, every statement tenant-filtered, `app.*` without API grants (steps 2–7, `176`).
  - *Costs reaching the waiter or the chef:* `order_line_cost` RLS owner/admin, `004`/`059` fixture rows (1); fire reply = M18 line shape (3); kitchen key scans (6, 13), strict outputs that 500 on injected keys (13, 14), components and e2e "no €" (18, 19); no new cost-named function (7).
  - *IDOR:* foreign session/line ids → P0002/404; a fire mixing sessions is refused whole (3).
  - *Input validation:* strict Zod + strict SQL (array bounds, reason list, ISO time with tolerance), body limits, JSON only (CSRF via `withApi`). Notes and names rendered as text (`<script>` case in 18).
  - *Personal data:* notes on tickets carry the M18 hint; codes-only logs; conflicts as results. GDPR register: no new category (project Q13/Q14).
- **Correctness**
  - *Double depletion:* line locks + skip rule + race `fire-twice` + integration `Promise.all`.
  - *Partial writes:* one transaction; `173` fault injection.
  - *Wrong batch:* FEFO with five candidate states (`171`); the same "live" as M18 (no "Ne restano" vs depletion mismatch).
  - *Shortage on a finished/discarded batch:* deliberate, alerted, fixed by a count; `177` and M15's `batch_state` check stay green (`137` already accepts finished below 0).
  - *Batch produced during a fire:* used from the next fire (§3.3, accepted).
  - *Breaking M12–M18:* every replaced object is listed (§2.1) and its tests named; `179` md5-pins the rest; the shape check is the union of M15's.
  - *Status change on purpose:* `166`/`169` updated in step 3 because the M17 stub is replaced (the M17/M18 plans foresaw it).
- **Unverified (proved in the named step):**
  - **T1:** a FOR UPDATE row lock whose `WHERE` reads `state` re-checks the updated row after waiting (EvalPlanQual), so a batch discarded while the fire waits is not drained (<https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/transaction-iso.html#XACT-READ-COMMITTED>). Proved by `fire-vs-discard` (11).
  - **T2:** `app.post_stock_outflow` with a lot override and `p_qty` equal to that lot's bucket writes exactly one row (reading of `20261026100400`). Proved in `171` (2).
  - **T3:** the generated `state` and M18's guard accept the fire update and the void update unchanged (reading of `20261101100000`). Proved in `172`/`174` (3, 4).
  - **T4:** Playwright's `page.clock` (`install`, `fastForward`) drives `setInterval` and `Date.now()` in the page, in Chromium and WebKit (<https://playwright.dev/docs/clock>; this session could not reach playwright.dev to re-read it). Proved by `kitchen.spec.ts` (19).
- **Performance / cost:** §3.10; `178` net; no figure promised; no provider or running cost beyond existing Supabase/Vercel usage (one kitchen read per 5 s per open kitchen screen).
- **Dependencies:** none added. Playwright `laptop` uses the installed Chromium.
- **Plan changes to record in DigiAlto-Ops `clients/osteria-dei-rossi/context.md`:**
  - §3.3: `POST /api/tables/:id/fire` → `/api/table-sessions/:id/fire`;
  - §3.5.2: `order_line_cost` readable by owner/admin only (Q16 = V6), with `total_cost`, `recipe_id`, `incomplete` and missing lists;
  - §3.5.3: `fire_course(p_session, {lineIds})`, `void_line(p_line, {reason, clientUpdatedAt})` (jsonb bodies as M18);
  - §3.1/§3.8: the kitchen screen polls every 5 s (instant updates are V2);
  - success metric "stock-outs < 2/week": counted from `negative_stock` alerts with `entity_type 'recipe_key'`.

- **Q97 How does "segue" work at the pass?**
  - **Default:** a segue dish stays on the waiter's phone until the waiter presses "Invia a seguire"; that is when the kitchen sees it and stock moves.

  - **Default:** yes (simpler, and the login token stays out of the browser).
  - **Alternative:** instant updates (→ V2).
- **Q99 Should the chef be able to send or call courses from the kitchen screen?**
  - **Default:** no; waiters (and you) send.
  - **Alternative:** yes (→ V3).
- **Q100 With two storage areas, where are a dish's raw ingredients (polenta, bread) taken from?**
  - **Default:** from the lot that expires first, wherever it is.
  - **Alternative:** always from one place you choose (→ V4).
- **Q101 A batch past its use-by but not thrown away: does the kitchen still serve from it?**
  - **Default:** no; the app counts only batches within their date (as the waiter's "Ne restano"), and anything more is a shortage.
  - **Alternative:** yes, after the in-date ones (→ V5).
- **Q102 Should "Invia" ask for confirmation?**
  - **Default:** no, one tap; a mistake is fixed with "Storna".
  - **Alternative:** a confirm step (→ V6).
- **Q103 Void reasons and how they count as waste.** Default: the six reasons and the mapping in §3.1.7 (e.g. "Errore in cucina" counts as plating error). Different wording or mapping is a constant change (`VOID_REASONS`, `app.order_void_reason_codes()`, parity test), not a variant.

- **Q105 What the kitchen list shows.** Default: today's sent dishes of open tables, oldest first, voided dishes struck through, no "done" button (that is the Phase 2 KDS, M40). Changing the order or the window is a constant change.

- **A1:** "Direct raw ingredients" include those inside a sub-recipe made per plate (a sauce), but never what is inside a batch (consumed at production).
