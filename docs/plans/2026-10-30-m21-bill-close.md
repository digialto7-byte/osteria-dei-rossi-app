## 1. Goal and scope

- **Goal:** every table is closed against its fiscal receipt with a bill computed once in the database, and each evening the owner compares the app's total with the register's total and sees which tables have no receipt number.
- **In scope:**
  - **Bill** (`app.session_bill_json`, `public.session_bill`): non-voided lines at `round(qty × unit_price, 2)` (half portions at 0,5 × price), coperto, VAT-inclusive total, split by covers (`parts`) or by items (groups + shared dishes), cent-exact; a `billToken` that changes with any line, covers or coperto change.
  - **`public.close_table(p_session, {receiptType, receiptNumber, paymentType, billToken})`:** single transaction, session FOR UPDATE, receipt type `scontrino`/`fattura`, optional number (`missing_receipt` flag), payment type, frozen `total`, `closed_business_date` from the Q18 cut-off; refuses unsent lines and stale previews; idempotent replay.
  - **`day_close`** (append-only) and **`public.enter_day_close`**, **`public.app_vs_register`** (live), owner and admin only, enforced in Postgres.
  - **API:** `GET /api/table-sessions/:id/bill`, `POST /api/table-sessions/:id/bill/split`, `POST /api/table-sessions/:id/close`, `POST /api/day-close`, `GET /api/reports/app-vs-register`.
  - **Screens** (phone first): "Conto" on the comanda (`/tavoli/comanda?s=`), the split panel, the close sheet, and the end-of-day screen `/chiusura` (owner, admin).
  - **M20 interplay:** close is online-only; the comanda blocks it while the phone has queued ops for the session; queued ops of other phones arriving after the close become `session_closed` conflicts (M20, unchanged) and are counted in the end-of-day view.
  - pgTAP `215`–`222`, six race cases, Vitest, an integration suite, Playwright on `phone` and `phone-webkit`.
- **Out of scope:**
  - payments, payment terminals, fiscal documents, receipt printing, the AdE corrispettivi cross-check (M35), any RCH integration (PRD "What not to buy");
  - reopening a closed table (closed is final; a new session can be opened at once, M17);
  - correcting a receipt number after close (Q132 → V4);
  - a register-total entry by the cashier (Q133 → V5);
  - an offline close (Q135 → V6);
  - revenue KPIs, net-of-VAT revenue and covers reports (M22 reads `total`, `closed_business_date` and the lines);
  - a configurable payment-type list (Q126: a constant change).

## 3. Design

### 3.1 Decisions

1. **One bill computation, in SQL** (PRD 3.4; AT2).
   - **Lines:** every line of the session with `state <> 'voided'` (drafts and fired). `amount = round(qty × unit_price, 2)` (Postgres `numeric` `round`, half away from zero): 0,5 × 14,00 = 7,00; 0,5 × 7,25 = 3,63; 1,5 × 12,50 = 18,75. Order: course enum, `created_at`, id.
   - **Coperto:** the session's frozen `coperto_total` (`covers × coperto_amount`), shown as `{perPerson, covers, amount}`; null when the session has none.
   - **Totals:** `subtotal = Σ amount`; `total = subtotal + coperto amount`. Prices are VAT-inclusive (ADR-016), so the total is VAT-inclusive; the bill shows "IVA inclusa" and **no VAT breakdown** (no fiscal document; M22 computes net revenue per line VAT rate).
   - **Unsent lines:** `unsentLineIds` lists the drafts; the preview marks them "Non inviato".
   - **`billToken`** = `md5` of `string_agg(id || ':' || state || ':' || qty || ':' || unit_price, ',' order by id)` of the non-voided lines `|| '|' || covers || '|' || coalesce(coperto_amount::text, '-')`. Any add, edit, removal, void, fire or covers change changes it.
   - **Money types:** every amount is `numeric(12,2)` in SQL. JSON carries them as numbers with at most two decimals, **for display only**: no component or `src/lib` helper adds, splits or rounds money. Money coming **in** (the register total) is a decimal **string** (decision 7).
2. **Splits, exact to the cent** (PRD 3.4; AT2).
   - **By covers:** `parts` n (2–99; the UI proposes the session's covers). In cents: `base = floor(T / n)`, `r = T − n × base`; parts 1…r get `base + 1`, the rest `base`. 100,00 € ÷ 3 → 33,34 + 33,33 + 33,33. `app.split_cents(p_cents bigint, p_parts int) returns bigint[]` is the one implementation.
   - **By items:** 2–20 groups, each `{lineIds, covers}`, plus `sharedLineIds`.
     - Every non-voided line of the session appears **exactly once** across the groups and the shared list.
     - `Σ covers` = the session's covers.
     - Group amount = Σ its line amounts + `covers × coperto_amount` + its share of the shared amount, which is split with `app.split_cents` over the groups in their given order.
     - Errors: P0001 `split_invalid`, DETAIL `{"reason": "line_missing" | "line_twice" | "line_unknown" | "covers_mismatch"}` (codes only). A voided line or another session's line counts as `line_unknown`.
   - **AT2 fixture:** 2 × Spezzatino 14,00 = 28,00; 0,5 × Spezzatino = 7,00; 1,5 × Tagliatelle 12,50 = 18,75; 1 × Vino 40,25; one Tagliatelle voided after firing (excluded); coperto 2,00 × 3 = 6,00 → **total 100,00**. By covers 3 → 33,34 / 33,33 / 33,33. By items, groups A {2 × Spezzatino, 1 cover}, B {Tagliatelle, 1}, C {½ Spezzatino, 1} and Vino shared → 4 025 cents ÷ 3 = 1 342 / 1 342 / 1 341 → A 43,42, B 34,17, C 22,41 (Σ 100,00).
   - A split is a computation, never stored. Each part's index is stable (`data-part-index`).
3. **`close_table`** (PRD 3.4; AT1, AT4).
   - **Body** `{receiptType, receiptNumber, paymentType, billToken}`, all four keys required:
     - `receiptType` ∈ `app.receipt_types()` = `{scontrino, fattura}` → else P0001 `receipt_type_invalid`;
     - `receiptNumber`: a JSON string or null. It is trimmed, and an empty string becomes null. Otherwise it must match `^[0-9A-Za-z][0-9A-Za-z/.-]{0,19}$` (RCH numbers such as "0012-0034"; no spaces) → else `receipt_number_invalid`. **Null is allowed for both types** (the PRD flags it, it does not refuse it);
     - `paymentType` ∈ `app.payment_type_codes()` = `{cash, card, other}` (Q126) → else `payment_type_invalid`;
     - `billToken` 32 lowercase hex → else 22023.
   - **Order of work** (one transaction): role → parse → session FOR UPDATE → closed? → drafts? → bill and token → write.
     - Already closed with the **same** receipt type, number and payment type → `{changed: false, bill}` (a lost reply retried). Otherwise → P0001 `session_closed`.
     - Any draft line → P0001 `unsent_lines`, DETAIL `{"lineIds": [...]}` (ids only, at most 50). Nothing is written (Q129 → V2).
     - The token differs from the bill now → P0001 `bill_changed`. The client re-reads the bill and asks again.
     - Write: `closed_at = v_now (clock_timestamp())`, `closed_by`, `closed_business_date = app.business_date(tenant, v_now)`, `receipt_type`, `receipt_number`, `payment_type`, `total` = the bill total, `updated_*`. `missing_receipt` is a generated column (`closed_at is not null and receipt_type is not null and receipt_number is null`).
     - Reply `{changed: true, bill}` with `bill.receipt` filled.
   - The work after the role check and parse is in `app.close_session_at(p_tenant, p_user, p_session, p_body, p_at timestamptz)` so pgTAP can close at a fixed instant (AT4); `public.close_table` calls it with `clock_timestamp()`.
   - **Effects on the session:** status `closed` (M17's derivation); it leaves the kitchen list (M19); the table can be opened again at once (M17). Every later write is refused: `update_table_session`, `add_order_line` and `fire_course` raise `session_closed`; `update_order_line`, `remove_order_line` and `void_line` return `{conflict: {reason: session_closed}}`; `open_table` with that session id raises `session_closed`. All of this is existing behaviour, re-asserted in `217` and `218`.
   - **Roles:** owner, admin, waiter (`app.require_table_operator()`); the chef gets 42501.
4. **Close and the offline queue** (M20; Q135 → V6).
   - **Close is online-only**, like every owner and kitchen write. It is **not** an outbox op kind: `app.sync_op_kinds()`, the dispatcher and `SYNC_KINDS` stay M20's six. This overrides the M20 §3.4 proposal (recorded in §6 plan changes).
   - **On the closing phone:** the close button is disabled when (a) the outbox state is `offline`/`backoff`/`signedOut`, or the last bill read failed by network, or (b) the outbox holds any op of this session (pending, waiting or held). The pure helper `closeBlock(outbox, sessionId)` returns `offline | queued | null`. Texts: "Serve la connessione per chiudere il tavolo." / "Ci sono 3 operazioni di questo tavolo ancora in coda su questo telefono: attendi l'invio." A queued fire of a draft is also caught on the server by `unsent_lines`.
   - **Queued ops of other phones that arrive after the close** follow M20's rule unchanged: "irreversible states win", so a closed table accepts nothing. `apply_op` stores them as `conflict` (`session_closed`), and the waiter who queued them sees M20's notice naming the dish. **Nothing reopens the table.** The dish is not in the app's total; if the register billed it, the end-of-day difference shows it.
   - **Visibility for the owner:** the report counts `lateOps` = `applied_op` rows of the business date (by `app.business_date(tenant, received_at)`) with outcome `conflict` and reason `session_closed`, whether the procedure raised it (`result.message`) or returned it (`result.conflict.reason`). The exact `result` shape is checked against M20's merged SQL (T2).
5. **Business date of a closed table** (Q18; AT4). `closed_business_date = app.business_date(tenant, closed_at)`: a table closed at 00:40 with the cut-off at 02:00 belongs to the previous business date; with a cut-off of 00:30 it belongs to the same calendar date. DST nights (2026-10-25, 2027-03-28) follow M11's `local_instant` rule. `business_date` (opening) is kept and not used by M21's report (Q128 → V1).
6. **App vs register report** (PRD 3.4; AT1, AT3).
   - `public.app_vs_register(p_date date)` (null = today's business date), owner and admin. It is computed **live** on each call, with no cache and no job.
   - **App:** sessions with `closed_business_date = D`: `total` = Σ `total`; `tables`; `covers`; `byReceiptType` and `byPaymentType` (`[{type, total, tables}]`, every code listed, zeros included).
   - **Register:** the latest `day_close` row of D (`created_at desc, id desc`) → `{total, enteredAt, enteredBy, entries}`; null when none.
   - **Difference:** `amount = app.total − register.total`; `pct = round(amount / register.total × 100, 1)`, null when the register total is 0 (Q131). Null when there is no register entry.
   - **`missingReceipts`:** the sessions of D with `missing_receipt`, ordered by `closed_at`: `{sessionId, tableName, covers, closedAt, closedBy {id, displayName}, receiptType, paymentType, total}`.
   - **`openTables`:** sessions still open whose opening `business_date ≤ D` (a table forgotten open shows up): `{count, provisionalTotal}` (the bill totals as of now).
   - **`lateOps`:** `{count}` (decision 4).
   - **Fatture are included in the app total** (the PRD compares "app total vs register total") and shown as their own subtotal in `byReceiptType` (Q130 → V3).
   - The date range accepted is from 62 days before today's business date to today's (`business_date_invalid` outside).
7. **`day_close` entry** (PRD 3.4; AT3, AT5).
   - `public.enter_day_close({businessDate?, registerTotal})`: owner and admin (`app.require_day_closer()`, 42501 for waiter and chef **before** any parse).
     - `registerTotal` must be a JSON **string** matching `^[0-9]{1,7}(\.[0-9]{1,2})?$` (0 to 9 999 999,99) → else P0001 `register_total_invalid`. A string keeps the value exact from the keyboard to `numeric`.
     - `businessDate` (ISO date, default today's) within the range of decision 6 → else `business_date_invalid`.
   - `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended('day_close:' || tenant || ':' || date, 0))`. If the latest row of that date has the same total, the reply is `{changed: false, report}`. Otherwise a new row is inserted and the reply is `{changed: true, report}`, with `report = app.app_vs_register_json(tenant, date)`. The difference is therefore in the very reply of the save (AT3), and `GET` returns the same object.
   - **Waiter and chef cannot write `day_close` in Postgres** in two ways: the procedure's role check, and no INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE grant and no write policy on the table for any API role (AT5).
8. **Receipt and payment codes (Q126).** `app.receipt_types()` = `{scontrino, fattura}`; `app.payment_type_codes()` = `{cash, card, other}`. Both are immutable SQL functions mirrored by `RECEIPT_TYPES` and `PAYMENT_TYPES` in TS, with a parity test. Labels: "Scontrino", "Fattura"; "Contanti", "Carta", "Altro" (English: "Receipt", "Invoice"; "Cash", "Card", "Other"). A different list is a constant change, not a variant. This deviates from the project plan's `settings.payment_types[]` (§6 plan changes).

### 3.2 Data model and procedures

**`20261110100000_close_core.sql`** (step 1)

### 3.3 Lock order (no deadlocks)

`applied_op advisory` (M20) → `dining_table` FOR UPDATE (open) / `table_session` FOR SHARE (line writers) **or FOR UPDATE (covers, close)** → `order_line` FOR UPDATE → stock advisory locks → batch rows → batch lot

| Procedure | Takes (in this order) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `add_order_line`, `update_order_line`, `remove_order_line`, `fire_course`, `void_line` | session SHARE → … | M18, M19 |
| `update_table_session` | session UPDATE | M17 |
| **`close_table`** | session UPDATE; then reads lines (no line lock: every line writer holds the session SHARE, so none can run) | M21 |
| **`session_bill`** | none (a read; the close re-checks with `billToken`) | M21 |
| **`enter_day_close`** | advisory `day_close:<tenant>:<date>` only | M21 |
| `apply_op` | op advisory → the procedure's own locks | M20 |

- `close_table` takes one row lock and nothing after it, so it adds no cycle. An add/fire that holds the session SHARE makes the close wait; afterwards the close sees the new line (`bill_changed`, or `unsent_lines` for a draft). A close that holds UPDATE makes the add wait; afterwards the add sees `closed_at` (`session_closed`). Races in step 10.

## 4. Alternatives considered

- **Close through M20's outbox (`close_table` op kind, M20 §3.4):** rejected as default (ADR-023): a queued close could freeze a total that misses a colleague's later dish, and the register needs the network anyway. Kept as V6 (Q135).
- **Compute the bill and splits in TypeScript:** rejected: floating point, and a second implementation next to the frozen total; CLAUDE.md forbids data logic in React.
- **Proportional split by items of the coperto:** rejected: covers per group is exact (`covers × coperto_amount`) and matches how a table pays.
- **Auto-fire drafts at close:** rejected as default (a forgotten draft would be charged and taken from stock); kept as V2 (Q129).
- **Refuse closing without a receipt number:** rejected by the PRD ("allowed but flagged").
- **Recompute totals at report time instead of storing `total`:** rejected: the total at close is the figure matched with the receipt (frozen like costs at sale); `218` proves it equals the recomputed bill.
- **A nightly invariant check `bill_total`:** rejected for M21: the guard and the session lock already make closed lines immutable; adding it would re-create `app.ledger_checks_body` and re-pin `196` for no reachable failure.
- **One mutable `day_close` row:** rejected: append-only keeps who entered which figure (ADR-002 spirit), at no cost.
- **`payment_types` in tenant settings (project §3.5.1):** rejected for M21: `tenant.settings` is readable by every member and no list is approved yet (Q126); a constant with a parity test is smaller. A settings list can come with the founder's answer.
- **VAT breakdown on the bill:** rejected: not required by the PRD for a non-fiscal preview; M22 computes net revenue.
- **Money as integer cents in every JSON:** rejected: the M17 coperto and M18 line shapes already use euro numbers; M21 keeps them for display and takes money input as a decimal string.

## 6. Risks and mitigations

- **Security / privacy**
  - *Definer functions bypass RLS:* role check first, empty `search_path`, tenant from `app.tenant_id()`, every statement tenant-filtered, `app.*` without API grants (steps 2–6, `220`).
  - *Waiter or chef writing `day_close`:* procedure role check + no write grant/policy + append-only trigger (steps 1, 5, 6); API 403 before any RPC (12).
  - *Costs reaching the waiter:* the bill holds sale prices only; key scans in SQL (`216`) and strict outputs that 500 on injected keys (11, 12); no cost-named function (`220`).
  - *IDOR:* foreign session ids → P0002/404; split ids of another session → `line_unknown` (2); tenant B's report never counts A (5, 6).
  - *Input validation:* strict Zod + strict SQL (receipt regex, code lists, decimal-string totals, date range, array bounds), body limits, JSON only (CSRF via `withApi`). Table and staff names rendered as text (React escaping).
  - *Personal data:* no guest data, no card numbers; DETAILs carry ids/codes only; logs carry codes. Retention project Q14; no new GDPR category.
- **Correctness**
  - *Split cents not adding up:* one integer-cent algorithm (`app.split_cents`) with remainder cases (2), AT2 everywhere.
  - *Closing a stale bill:* `billToken` + session FOR UPDATE (3, races 10).
  - *Dishes never depleted:* `unsent_lines` refusal (3); a queued fire on the closing phone blocks the button (11, 15).
  - *Dishes queued on another phone after close:* refused by M20's rule, shown on that phone, counted as `lateOps` (4, 5). **Accepted:** the app total misses them; the difference shows it.
  - *Breaking M17–M20:* only the guard and the purge list are replaced, with the named test changes (1, 7); `222` md5-pins the rest; legacy closed fixtures stay valid (one-directional check).
- **Unverified (proved in the named step):**
  - **T1:** a `generated always … stored` column may reference `closed_at`, `receipt_type` and `receipt_number` of the same row and is recomputed on UPDATE (<https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/ddl-generated-columns.html>; not re-read in this session). Proved in `215` (1).
  - **T2:** M20's merged `applied_op.result` for a raised P0001 conflict is `{sqlstate, message, detail}` and for a returned conflict is the procedure reply `{conflict: {reason, line}}` (M20 plan §3.2, read from the M20 worktree file, not from the merged SQL). Proved in `218`/`219` (4, 5); if the merged shape differs, `app.late_op_count` follows the code.
  - **T3:** `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended(…, 0))` serialises two `enter_day_close` calls for one date (the M20 pattern). Proved by race `day-close-twice` (10).
- **Performance / cost:** §3.10; `221` net; no figure promised; no provider or running cost beyond existing Supabase/Vercel usage.
- **Dependencies:** none added.
- **Plan changes to record in DigiAlto-Ops `clients/osteria-dei-rossi/context.md`:**
  - §3.3: `GET /api/tables/:id/bill` → `GET /api/table-sessions/:id/bill` (+ `POST …/bill/split`); `POST /api/tables/:id/close` → `POST /api/table-sessions/:id/close`;
  - §3.5.1: `table_session` gains `closed_business_date` (the business date of the close; `business_date` stays the opening date), `total` frozen at close, `missing_receipt` generated; `payment_types` is a fixed list (`cash`, `card`, `other`), not a tenant setting (Q126);
  - §3.5.2: `day_close` is append-only; the latest entry of a date is in force;
  - §3.8 / M20 §3.4: **close is online-only and not an outbox op kind** (ADR-023; V6 restores the M20 contract if Q135 says so).

  - **Default:** no coperto until you set one in "Tavoli · impostazioni" (M17). The tests use an invented 2,00 € at 10 %, which is not a fact about the osteria.
- **Q128 On which evening does a table closed after the cut-off count?**
  - **Default:** on the business day of the **close** (when the receipt is printed): closed at 00:40 → the previous evening; closed at 02:40 → the new day.
  - **Alternative:** on the evening it was **opened** (→ V1).
- **Q129 What happens to dishes entered but never sent to the kitchen when the table is closed?**
  - **Default:** the table cannot be closed until they are sent or removed.
  - **Alternative:** closing sends them automatically (→ V2).

  - **Default:** yes; the app compares all closed tables and shows the fattura subtotal apart.
  - **Alternative:** fatture are outside the register (→ V3).
- **Q131 How should the difference read?**
  - **Default:** app minus register, in € and as % of the register total ("L'app registra 12,50 € in più della cassa").
  - Another sign or base is a constant change.
- **Q132 Can a receipt number be added or corrected after the table is closed?**
  - **Default:** no in M21; the table stays flagged in the end-of-day list.
  - **Alternative:** you or the admin can fix it, with a record of the change (→ V4).
- **Q133 Who enters the register total at the end of the day?** (project Q8)
  - **Default:** you or the admin.
  - **Alternative:** a staff member at the register, seeing only the total and the difference (→ V5, project V3 adapted).
- **Q134 Splitting by dishes: shared dishes and coperto.**
  - **Default:** shared dishes (e.g. a bottle of wine) are split equally between the groups, with the extra cent going to the first groups; each group pays the coperto of the covers it has.
  - Another rule is a constant change.
- **Q135 Must a table be closable without a connection?**
  - **Default:** no. Closing needs the connection, like the register, and the button waits until that phone has sent its queued orders. Orders from another phone that arrive after the close are refused, shown to that waiter and counted in the end-of-day view; the table is not reopened.
