## 1. Goal and scope
- **Goal:** one tap by the owner turns a reviewed invoice into stock receipts, lots, price history and a payable, in a single Postgres transaction that either writes everything or nothing, and every invoice that is not right (total mismatch, duplicate, unmapped stock lines) is refused with a clear Italian reason.
- **In scope:**
  - tables `audit_event`, `raw_lot`, `stock_movement` (type `receipt` only), `price_history`, `payable`; append-only triggers (UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE raise for every role); view `v_stock_on_hand`;
  - `public.approve_invoice(p_invoice, p_location)`: one transaction with gates (status `review`, no total mismatch, not a duplicate, no open duplicate warning, supplier linked, every active stock line mapped and convertible to its ingredient's base unit, due date resolvable, location resolvable);
  - unit conversion of every receipt to the ingredient base unit (pack conversion for `conf` lines), price per base unit, credited value, due date from the invoice or the supplier's payment terms, payable amount = gross total − credited value;
  - `supplier_item.last_price` (the M1 column reserved for M5);
  - `public.reject_invoice(p_invoice, p_reason)`; the M4 duplicate override now writes an `audit_event`;
  - `public.invoice_approval_preview(p_invoice)`: the same computation, read-only, so the review screen shows the blockers, the payable and the quantities before the tap;
  - `POST /api/invoices/:id/approve`, `POST /api/invoices/:id/reject`; the review payload gains `approval`, `decision` and `posting`;
  - an approval panel and a reject form on `/fatture/[id]/revisione` (Italian, `1.234,50 €`, dates Europe/Rome);
  - four new two-session cases in `scripts/test-db-races.sh`.
- **Out of scope:**
  - price variance alert (M8); moving average and recipe costs (M8);
  - other movement types (consumption, waste, transfer, count, reversal) and `GET /api/stock` (M12); FEFO consumption (M12);
  - payable status changes, reminders, supplier statement, separate credit-note documents (M9);
  - undoing an approval (Q8 → V8);
  - per-delivery-note dates (Q1 → V2), negative lines (Q3 → V4), per-line locations (Q7 → V7).

## 3. Design

### 3.1 Decisions
1. **All of approval is one `SECURITY DEFINER` plpgsql procedure** (`search_path = ''`, tenant from `app.tenant_id()`, user from `app.app_user_id()`, every query filtered by tenant, role checked first). The route only calls it and reloads the review. No ledger INSERT grant exists for `authenticated` or `service_role`.
2. **One computation, two callers.** `app.approval_lines(p_invoice)` (per-line quantities, prices, credits) and `app.approval_blockers(p_invoice)` (the list of reasons not to approve) are used both by the read-only `invoice_approval_preview` (review screen) and by `approve_invoice`. The screen never computes amounts; the numbers shown are the numbers posted.
3. **Who approves:** owner and admin (role matrix §3.11.2). A separate `app.require_invoice_approver()` holds the list. The founder confirmed (2026-10-22) that the chef neither reviews nor approves and never sees € costs (M4 V3 is dropped); keeping the approver check separate still means a future change to the review list cannot silently grant approval.
4. **Gate order** (approve raises the first that applies; the preview lists all of them):

   | # | Code | HTTP | Condition |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 0 | `not_found` (P0002) | 404 | not this tenant's invoice |
   | 1 | `invoice_already_approved` | 409 | status `approved` |
   | 2 | `invoice_rejected` | 409 | status `rejected` |
   | 3 | `duplicate_invoice` | 409 | status `duplicate`, or (no `duplicate_override`) another live invoice with the same VAT + number + date exists, checked under the duplicate advisory lock |
   | 4 | `invoice_not_in_review` | 409 | any other status (`received`, `extracting`, `extraction_failed`) |
   | 5 | `duplicate_warning` | 409 | `duplicate_warning_of` set and not overridden |
   | 6 | `total_mismatch` | 409 | `app.totals_gate` computed now is flagged (never trusts the stored flag alone) |
   | 7 | `supplier_required` | 422 | no `supplier_id` |
   | 8 | `invoice_date_required` / `invoice_date_in_future` | 422 | `invoice_date` null / after today in the tenant time zone |
   | 9 | `due_date_required` | 422 | no `due_date` on the invoice and no `payment_terms_days` on the supplier |
   | 10 | `lines_unmapped` | 422 | an active stock line has no ingredient |
   | 11 | `line_base_qty_unavailable` | 422 | an active mapped stock line with received qty > 0 has no conversion to its ingredient base unit (§3.2) |
   | 12 | `credit_vat_rate_missing` | 422 | an active line with `credit_qty > 0` has no `vat_rate` |
   | 13 | `payable_negative` | 422 | credited value > gross total |
   | 14 | `no_location` / `location_required` / `location_not_found` | 422 | no active location / several and none chosen / chosen one inactive or not the tenant's |

   `low_confidence` and `supplier_is_buyer` stay informational and do not block. Blockers 10–12 carry the line ids in the preview.
5. **Receipt date (base plan, Q1):** every receipt, lot and price row of an invoice carries `invoice.invoice_date` as its business date (`effective_on`, `received_on`). The approval moment is `created_at`. → V1, V2.
6. **Due date:** the invoice's `due_date` (extracted or corrected in the header editor) wins; else `invoice_date + supplier.payment_terms_days` (calendar days); else blocker 9. `payable.due_date_source` records `invoice` or `supplier_terms`. → V6.
7. **Payable amount:** `gross_amount = round(gross_total, 2)`; `credited_amount = Σ` per active line of `round(credit_qty × line_amount ÷ qty × (1 + vat_rate ÷ 100), 2)`; `amount = gross_amount − credited_amount`. The supplier is paid VAT too, so the payable is gross and the credit includes its VAT. → V5. Non-stock lines count in the gross like any line (they are on the invoice) and their credits count too.
8. **Location:** one per invoice. `p_location` null and exactly one active location → that one; none → `no_location`; several → `location_required` (the screen shows a select). → V7.
9. **`supplier_item.last_price`** = the price per base unit of the newest `price_history` row of that item (by `effective_on`, then `seq`), recomputed from the ledger inside the approval after the item rows are locked. An older invoice approved late never overwrites a newer price. Split children have no item and leave it unchanged.
10. **Approved and rejected invoices are frozen:** a trigger refuses any UPDATE of an `invoice_line` whose invoice is `approved` or `rejected` (`invoice_line_frozen`, every role), so the lines always match what was posted.
11. **No undo, no delete.** Corrections after approval wait for M12 reversals (`reverse_movement`) and M9 payable status (`disputed`). → V8.
12. **Reject** is allowed from `review` and `duplicate`, with an optional reason. It is final (the guard allows nothing out of `rejected`); a rejected invoice leaves the dedup index, so the same document can be uploaded again.
13. **No stock endpoint in M5.** `v_stock_on_hand` is created and tested; `GET /api/stock` with its role rules is M12. The approved review shows what each line posted.

### 3.2 Unit conversion, prices and credits (per active line)
`f` = base units per one line unit, from `app.base_factor(unit, base_unit, pack_count, pack_qty, pack_unit)`:
- `kg`, `l`, `pz` → 1; `g`, `ml` → 0.001; only when the unit's dimension equals the ingredient's base unit, else null;
- `conf` → `pack_count × pack_qty × f(pack_unit)` from the line's `supplier_item` as it is at approval (the M1 trigger guarantees the pack dimension equals the ingredient's), else null;
- null unit → null.

| Line | Receipt (`raw_lot` + `stock_movement`) | `price_history` | Credit (payable) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original stock line, measure unit | `qty_received = round((qty − credit_qty) × f, 4)` | `unit_price = round(line_amount ÷ (qty × f), 4)` € per base unit, net of VAT | §3.1.7 formula |
| Original stock line, `conf` | same, `f` from the pack: 2 `conf` of 6 × 1 kg → 12 kg | 30,00 € → 2,5000 €/kg | same |
| Measure unit in grams | 500 g on a kg ingredient → 0,5 kg | 4,00 € → 8,0000 €/kg | same |
| Line with a credit | 2 kg, credit 0,5 → 1,5 kg received | 20,00 € → 10,0000 €/kg (the unit price does not change) | 0,5 × 10,00 × 1,10 = 5,50 € |
| Fully credited (`credit_qty = qty`) or `qty = 0` | nothing (received 0) | nothing (no purchase) | credit counted |
| Split parent | nothing; its active children post | nothing | its `credit_qty` is 0 (M4 rule) |
| Active split child | as an original line, with its own unit and ingredient; `conf` impossible (no item) → blocker 11 | row with `supplier_item_id` null; `last_price` untouched | its own credit |
| Superseded child | nothing | nothing | nothing |
| Non-stock line | nothing | nothing | its own credit |
| Unit null, dimension mismatch (e.g. `pz` on a kg ingredient), `conf` without a pack | blocker 11 `line_base_qty_unavailable` | — | — |

The movement's `unit_cost` equals the price row's `unit_price` (cost frozen at receipt). `raw_lot` stores `source_qty`, `source_unit` and `base_factor`, so later pack edits never change what a receipt meant. The line price uses `line_amount ÷ qty`, not `unit_price`: it is what was charged, it exists when `unit_price` is null, and split children already have amounts. SQL `round` on numeric rounds half away from zero, which equals the TS half-up `round4` for these non-negative values; a parity test pins it (step 4).

**`20261022140500_approve_invoice.sql`**
- `public.invoice_approval_preview(p_invoice uuid, p_location uuid default null) returns jsonb` (definer, stable, owner/admin, tenant filtered, no locks, any status): `{status, blockers (empty unless review), receiptDate, payable {grossAmount, creditedAmount, amount, dueDate, dueDateSource}, receipts (count), lines [{lineId, receivedQty, baseUnit, unitPrice, creditedAmount}], locations {required, options [{id, name}], defaultId}}`.
- `public.approve_invoice(p_invoice uuid, p_location uuid default null) returns jsonb` (definer, volatile). Order:
  1. `app.require_invoice_approver()`; lock the invoice `for update` (tenant filtered, any status; P0002 if absent); status gates 1–4; take `pg_advisory_xact_lock(app.invoice_dup_lock_key(…))`; `v_blockers := app.approval_blockers(...)`; raise P0001 with the first code if any.
  2. `v_lines := array(select … from app.approval_lines(p_invoice))`: one read, used by every insert (one consistent view of lines and packs).
  3. Lock the `supplier_item` rows of `v_lines` `for update` **ordered by id**.
  4. Insert `raw_lot` and `stock_movement` (one data-modifying CTE) for stock lines with `received_qty > 0`, ordered by position and sub-position; `effective_on`/`received_on` = `app.receipt_date`, location from `app.resolve_location`.
  5. Insert `price_history` for stock lines with `received_qty > 0`, same order (so `seq` follows the invoice).
  6. `update supplier_item set last_price = (newest price_history row of the item)` for the locked items.
  7. Insert `payable` (§3.1.7, due date from `app.resolve_due_date`).
  8. `update invoice set status = 'approved'` (the guard allows it).
  9. Insert `audit_event` `invoice.approved` with `before {status}` and `after {status, payable_id, amount, due_date, due_date_source, receipt_date, location_id, receipts}`.
  10. Return `{payableId, amount, dueDate, receipts}`.
  The M8 variance check will be added after step 6.
- Execute granted to `authenticated` only (revoked from `public, anon, service_role`).

**`20261022140600_reject_invoice.sql`**

- `create or replace public.review_clear_duplicate`: unchanged behaviour, plus an `audit_event` `invoice.duplicate_cleared` (`before {status, duplicate_of, duplicate_warning_of}`).

### 3.4 Concurrency
- **One lock order everywhere:** the invoice row first. Every review procedure (`app.lock_review_invoice`), `approve_invoice` and `reject_invoice` take `FOR UPDATE` on the invoice before reading its lines. Under READ COMMITTED a waiting `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` re-reads the newest row version, and each later statement takes a new snapshot, so:
  - **double approval:** the second call waits, then sees `approved` → 409 `invoice_already_approved`, nothing written; the unique keys on `payable.invoice_id`, `raw_lot.invoice_line_id`, `price_history.invoice_line_id` and the receipt index are the backstop;
  - **edit committed first:** approval waits, then reads the edited lines (step 2 runs after the lock);
  - **approval committed first:** the edit waits, then `lock_review_invoice` sees `approved` → 409 `invoice_not_in_review`;
  - **approve vs reject:** the second sees the first's status → 409.
- **Propagation** (`automap_lines` with an item) skips locked invoices and only writes `review` invoices, so it never waits on, nor writes to, an invoice being approved.
- **Supplier items:** approval locks the items it uses ordered by id, after its own invoice. A review action holds its invoice and one item and never waits for another invoice (propagation is SKIP LOCKED); a direct item UPDATE is one statement. Two approvals sharing items lock them in the same order. So no wait cycle exists. The `last_price` recompute runs after the item locks, so it sees any approval that committed while we waited.
- **Duplicate key:** the advisory lock is the one extraction and `recheck_duplicate` use, so a concurrent extraction of the same document cannot slip past the live-duplicate check.
- **New two-session cases** in `scripts/test-db-races.sh` (step 8): `double-approve`, `edit-then-approve`, `approve-then-edit`, `crossed-items` (two invoices sharing two items in opposite line order, approved together; both commit and `last_price` follows invoice date, not commit order).

## 4. Alternatives considered
- **Approval logic in TypeScript with several queries:** rejected (ADR-002): a crash between inserts leaves partial ledgers, and roles would be enforced only in the API.
- **Stored on-hand balances updated at approval:** rejected by invariant 1; `v_stock_on_hand` derives them.
- **A separate `GET /api/invoices/:id/approval` endpoint:** rejected; the preview inside the review payload refreshes with every edit for free, and one round trip fewer.
- **Computing the preview in TS (extending `baseQtyOf`):** rejected; two implementations of money and quantity would drift. The SQL is the one implementation; TS keeps only the M4 base-quantity preview, pinned to SQL by the parity test.
- **Price from `unit_price` instead of `line_amount ÷ qty`:** rejected; `unit_price` can be null and may be printed before a line discount.
- **`last_price` as a view over `price_history` instead of the column:** rejected for now; the M1 schema reserved the column for M5 and approval writes it from the ledger in the same transaction, so it cannot drift from `price_history` except by an UPDATE no API role can make. Revisit in M8 if the cost engine reads only `price_history`.
- **Receipt date = approval date or per delivery note:** not the base plan, see Q1 (V1, V2).
- **`GET /api/stock` now:** deferred to M12, which owns stock endpoints and their role rules.
- **Undo by setting the invoice back to `review` and deleting rows:** rejected by invariant 5 (nothing is deleted); a reversal is V8.
- **Reusing `app.lock_review_invoice` in approval:** rejected; it raises `invoice_not_in_review` for a `duplicate`, and AT2 needs `duplicate_invoice`.

## 6. Risks and mitigations
- **Security / privacy**
  - *Definer procedures bypass RLS:* role checked first, `search_path = ''`, every query filtered by the invoice's tenant, execute for `authenticated` only; chef, waiter and owner-B cases in steps 6 and 7.
  - *Waiters never see costs:* no waiter policy on any M5 table; `004` fixture rows (steps 1–3); review payload string scan for `last_price` and `unit_cost` (step 10); integration check that the waiter reads 0 rows (step 12).
  - *Chef and costs (founder decision 2026-10-22):* invoice review and approval are owner/admin only, and the chef never sees € costs. So the chef has no SELECT on `stock_movement` (it carries `unit_cost`), `price_history`, `payable` or `audit_event`, and `v_stock_on_hand` returns the chef 0 rows; the chef reads `raw_lot` (no cost). M12 adds a cost-free stock path for the chef's stock screens (a definer function or a view without `unit_cost`).
  - *Free-text rejection reason:* capped at 200 characters, control and bidi characters refused, rendered escaped, hint against personal data, never logged.
  - *Logs and errors:* ids and codes only; database messages never reach the client (step 9).
  - *CSRF:* JSON-only writes with SameSite cookies (existing `withApi`).
  - *Append-only bypass:* a superuser can disable triggers (`session_replication_role = replica`) or drop them in a migration. No API role can; migrations are reviewed. Whether the hosted Supabase `postgres` role is a superuser is unverified (T3).
- **Correctness**
  - *Deferred invoices (Q1):* with the default, receipts for goods delivered weeks earlier carry the invoice date; stock is understated until approval and lots without expiry cannot be ordered by real delivery. **For M12:** do not derive an expiry from `default_shelf_life_days` + `received_on` for such lots without the owner's confirmation. **For M13:** a receipt dated inside a month already closed by a count changes that month; M13 must decide whether approval refuses or re-dates such receipts.
  - *Packs are mutable:* the factor used is frozen in `raw_lot.base_factor`; the preview shows it before the tap.
  - *Rounding:* receipts round to 4 decimals of the base unit; credits round per line to cents; the payable is exact by construction (check constraint).
  - *Stock line with `qty 0` and an amount:* posts nothing; the M4 line check flags the amount, which the owner should fix before approving.
  - *Approval is irreversible in M5:* the confirm step states it; Q8 asks whether a reversal is wanted (V8).
- **Unverified (checked in the named step):**
  - **T1** A waiting `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` re-reads the newest row version under READ COMMITTED (PostgreSQL behaviour relied on by M4 too): step 8 races.
  - **T2** `security_invoker` views apply the caller's RLS on the Supabase Postgres version: step 2 (`031` waiter reads 0 rows).
  - **T3** Whether the hosted `postgres` role can bypass triggers: not tested; the local migration-owner cases in `037` prove the trigger fires for the table owner.
  - **T4** A temporary trigger created inside a pgTAP transaction fires for the definer procedure: step 6 (AT4).
  - **T5** `pg_stat_xact_user_tables` counters inside a pgTAP transaction show the procedure's writes (as M4 T5); if not, step 6 compares row counts of every public table before and after.
- **Performance / cost:** a handful of indexed inserts per invoice line at about 25 invoices a month (unverified); `v_stock_on_hand` aggregates the ledger on each read, acceptable at this volume (ADR-002 names the revisit trigger). No performance figure is promised; no new running cost.
- **Dependencies:** none added.
- **Plan changes to record in DigiAlto-Ops `context.md`:**
  - `audit_event` arrives with actions `invoice.approved`, `invoice.rejected`, `invoice.duplicate_cleared`;
  - ledger rows carry a business date (`effective_on`, `received_on`) separate from `created_at`; `price_history.effective_at` (plan §3.5.1) becomes `effective_on` (a date), plus `seq`, `supplier_item_id` and `base_unit`;
  - `raw_lot` stores the conversion it used (`source_qty`, `source_unit`, `base_factor`);
  - `payable` stores `gross_amount`, `credited_amount`, `due_date_source`; credits include VAT;
  - `supplier_item.last_price` is € per ingredient base unit, newest by invoice date;
  - the approval preview lives in the review payload; no `GET /api/stock` until M12;
  - lines of approved or rejected invoices are frozen by a trigger;
  - approval has its own role check (`app.require_invoice_approver`).

## 7. Open questions for the founder
- **Decided (founder, 2026-10-22):** invoice review and approval are owner/admin only; the chef does not review and never sees € costs (`app.require_invoice_reviewer` unchanged; `app.require_invoice_approver` owner/admin). M4 Q3 is closed.
- **Q1 Receipt date when one invoice covers many delivery notes.** Example from the sample you described: 11 delivery notes dated 1 to 26 September, invoiced on 29 September, each delivery-note header printed inside the invoice (today they are not read as lines). Which date should the stock receipts and the price history use?
  - **(a) Invoice date — the plan's default.** Stock on hand today is right once the invoice is approved. Before approval, those goods are missing from stock (true of every option). All 11 deliveries look as if they arrived on 29/09: September totals are right, but stock on a date in early September is understated; lots with no printed expiry cannot be told apart by delivery for "first expiring, first out" (FEFO); any expiry worked out from the receipt date would be up to 4 weeks too late, so M12 will not do that for these lots without asking you. Prices of the whole month are recorded on 29/09.
  - **(b) Approval date (→ V1).** Same as (a), but if you approve in October, September's stock reconciliation shows a shortage and October a surplus; prices move to the approval day.
  - **(c) Each delivery note's own date (→ V2).** Correct dates for stock, FEFO order and prices. It needs a change to invoice reading (M3: read the delivery-note number and date for each line), a field on the review screen (M4), and re-checking the sample invoices; reading accuracy for the new field is measured on the samples.
- **Q2 Totals check: gross or net (open since M3, plan Q20).** The check compares the sum of the lines plus VAT with the invoice total (gross); the sample you described supports this.
  - **Default (gross):** approval is refused while the lines plus VAT differ from the total by more than €0.05; the payable starts from the gross total.
  - **Net (→ V3, with M3 V1 and M4 V2):** the lines are compared with the taxable amount; approval also refuses an invoice whose taxable + VAT differs from the gross; the payable still starts from the gross, because that is what you pay.
- **Q3 Negative lines (discounts, deposits, returns), open since M3.** Today such an invoice fails at reading, so it never reaches approval. **If they appear (→ V4, after M3 V2 and M4 V1):** the line is booked as non-stock with a cost centre ("Sconti", "Cauzioni"), it creates no stock or price row, and the payable is the printed total, which already includes it. Consequence: a discount printed as a separate line does not lower the ingredient's price, so food cost is slightly overstated. **Separate credit notes** (note di credito) are not handled in M5; they belong with the supplier statement (M9) or later. Do your suppliers send them?
- **Q4 Remember non-stock lines per supplier (M4 Q6).** At approval nothing changes either way: non-stock lines create no stock movement and no price row; their cost centre is already required by the review (a line cannot be non-stock without one). The answer only decides whether the review pre-fills them (M4 V4).
- **Q5 Payable and short deliveries.** The payable is what you owe the supplier including VAT: gross total minus the credited value of short or damaged deliveries.
  - **Default:** the credit includes its VAT (1 kg missing at 10,00 € with 10% VAT → 11,00 € less to pay), so a credited line needs a VAT rate.
  - **Alternative (→ V5):** the credit is at the net price (10,00 €), and the VAT difference is settled with the supplier separately.
  - A payable net of VAT is not offered, because the supplier is paid the VAT too; say so if your accountant wants it differently.
- **Q6 Due date when neither the invoice nor the supplier gives one.** The printed (or corrected) due date wins; else invoice date + the supplier's payment days.
  - **Default:** approval is refused with "Manca la scadenza" until you type it in the invoice header. Consequence: one extra field for suppliers without terms.
  - **Alternative (→ V6):** the due date becomes the invoice date, and the reminder fires immediately.
  - Also: terms like "60 gg fine mese" (end of month) are not understood; supplier payment days count from the invoice date. Do any of your suppliers use end-of-month terms?
- **Q7 Where deliveries are stored.** One storage location per invoice (default): chosen automatically when only one location exists, asked at approval when several do. If one delivery is split between, say, the cold room and the dry store, choose per line (→ V7).
- **Q8 Undoing an approval.** The rules say nothing is deleted, only reversed.
  - **Default:** no undo in M5. A mistake after approval is fixed later with stock corrections (M12) and by marking the payable "disputed" (M9).
  - **Alternative (→ V8):** "Annulla approvazione" posts a reversal of every receipt and price, cancels the payable and keeps the history.
- **Assumptions (confirm):**
  - "low confidence" readings and the "supplier = buyer" warning do not block approval;
  - a fully credited line (all returned) creates no stock and no price row;
  - the price per kg (or litre, or piece) is the line amount divided by the quantity, net of VAT;
  - a rejected invoice cannot be reopened; upload it again if needed;
  - an invoice dated in the future cannot be approved.
