# ADR-016 — Menu and tables: a mutable menu item with an append-only price history, coperto outside tenant settings and frozen per cover at opening, one open session per table with a derived status

- **Status:** proposed
- **Date:** 2026-10-24
- **Decided by:** architect.
  - The decision stays within ADR-002 (derived balances, append-only ledgers), ADR-003 (RLS, roles in Postgres), ADR-005 (offline queue: client-generated ids, idempotent replay), ADR-007 (immutable recipe versions, `recipe_key` as the stable identity) and ADR-008 (cost engine; costs frozen at sale).
  - It applies the founder decision in force: Q16 = project V6 (the chef never sees €).
  - The M17 plan `docs/plans/2026-10-24-m17-menu-tables.md` §7 Q68–Q74 are open, with their defaults in force.

## Context
- **PRD 3.1:** a table list with status (free, open, courses sent, bill requested, closed), covers, waiter and open time; covers entered at opening; coperto applied automatically if configured; several waiters on one table.
- **PRD data model:** `menu_item (name, category, course_default, recipe_id, price, vat_rate, is_available, is_pushed, push_until)` and `table_session (table_name, covers, waiter_id, opened_at, closed_at, …)`.
- **Project plan:** §3.5.1 (menu_item references `recipe_key`; `push_price` for M23; table_session `coperto_total`, `business_date`; ids generated by the client); §3.6 invariants (nothing deleted, prices and costs frozen at sale); §3.11.2 (waiters never see costs); §5a.3 M17 AT1–AT5 and the consumers M18–M23.
- **What exists (SQL wins over plans):**
  - M7: `recipe_key` and immutable `recipe` versions; `recipe_price.sale_price` per plate **version** (a price change is a new version).
  - M8: `app.plate_cost_rows` takes the price from `recipe_price` and one tenant VAT rate `sale_vat_pct`. Its plan records the contract "M17: the menu item's VAT rate (and price) replace these for mapped plates".
  - M8 SQL comment (`20261023110000_cost_settings.sql`): **tenant settings hold no euro value**, because every member of the tenant, the chef included, reads the `tenant` row.
  - The role scans `004` and `059` fail if a waiter- or chef-readable column is named like `price`, `amount` or `total`, or if a new public function named `cost|margin|price` exists without its 42501 case.
  - M15: `app.business_date(tenant, at)` with the cut-off; M11: staff sessions.

## Decision
- **A menu item is one mutable row; its price is an append-only history.**
  - `menu_item` holds name, category, default course, the nullable `recipe_key` (null = `unmapped`), position, `is_available`, `is_active` and the M23 push fields (`push_until`, `push_price`). Every change writes an `audit_event`; nothing is deleted (deactivation).
  - `menu_item_price (menu_item_id, price, vat_rate, valid_from)` is append-only: a price or VAT change inserts a row. The price in force at an instant is the newest row not after it.
  - Sales freeze the price on the order line (M18, `order_line.unit_price` and its VAT rate). The history serves margins "as of a date" and the audit trail.
  - For a plate that an active menu item maps, `app.plate_cost_rows` uses the menu price and VAT rate in force at the date. Otherwise it keeps `recipe_price` and `sale_vat_pct`. At most one active menu item maps a given recipe key (Q69).
- **Coperto lives in its own owner/admin table `sale_settings`, not in `tenant.settings`.** At opening, `open_table` copies the per-cover amount and its VAT rate into the session. `table_session.coperto_total` is a generated column, `covers × coperto_amount`, and is null when the coperto is not set. Later setting changes never touch open or closed sessions. M21's bill reads the session columns.
- **A table session is opened once per table, safely under concurrency.**
  - `dining_table` holds the configured names (project Q5, entered on the settings screen).
  - `open_table(table, session_id, covers)` locks the table row and checks for an open session. A partial unique index `(tenant_id, dining_table_id) where closed_at is null` is the backstop.
  - The same `session_id` again is an idempotent replay (200). Any other session on an open table → 409 `table_already_open`.
  - Session ids come from the client (M20 offline).
- **The status is derived on every read, never stored:** closed > bill requested > courses sent > open > free.
  - "Courses sent" comes from `app.session_fired_lines(tenant, session_ids)`. M17 ships it as a stub returning no rows. M19 replaces it, same signature, with the count of fired order lines. The status function never changes.
- **Waiters read menu and tables only through security-definer functions with fixed, cost-free shapes.** No RLS policy on these tables names the waiter or the chef. The chef gets 403 on the menu and tables endpoints in M17 (the kitchen uses M19's tickets; Q70 → plan V3).

## Alternatives
- **Versioned menu items (immutable rows, a `menu_item_key`).** Pros: full history of every field. Cons: order lines, pushes and availability would reference a key and resolve versions, for fields (name, category, position) that have no reporting meaning. Rejected; the price is the only field that needs history.
- **Mutable price on `menu_item`, history only in `audit_event`.** Pros: one table fewer. Cons: margins "as of a date" would need to parse audit JSON, or would use today's price for past dates. Rejected.
- **The menu price derived from `recipe_price` for mapped plates.** Pros: one price per plate. Cons: every menu price change becomes a recipe version (cost and label churn, ADR-007), and unmapped items (drinks) still need their own price. Rejected. `recipe_price` stays the fallback for plates not on the menu.
- **`coperto_amount` in `tenant.settings` (the plan's wording).** Pros: no new table. Cons: the chef reads the tenant row, which breaks Q16 = V6 and the M8 rule. Rejected.
- **Coperto as an order line or a special menu item.** Pros: the bill sums lines only. Cons: it would go through the kitchen and firing flows (M18–M19), and covers changes would have to edit lines. Rejected.
- **A stored status column.** Pros: a cheap read. Cons: it duplicates facts held by the session and the order lines, and drifts when M19 fires or voids. Rejected (ADR-002 spirit).
- **Waiter RLS policies on the tables.** Pros: direct PostgREST reads. Cons: price-named columns become waiter-readable, the `059` scan fails, and every later column must be vetted. Rejected; definer reads with tested key sets.

## Consequences
- **Good:**
  - The waiter payloads have a fixed, snapshot-tested shape with no cost or margin key.
  - Opening a table is race-safe and replay-safe for M20.
  - M21 finds the coperto on the session. M19 plugs into the status without touching it.
  - Margins use the price the menu actually charges.
- **Bad / accepted trade-offs:**
  - Two price sources exist. Menu wins for mapped plates; the recipe form's price remains the fallback for plates not on the menu.
  - Margins for a past date use the menu item's current active state (an item deactivated today falls back to the recipe price for past dates).
  - `app.plate_cost_rows` is replaced (not md5-pinned today; M8's tests stay green unchanged).
  - The chef cannot see the menu or the tables in M17.
- **What would make us revisit this:**
  - several menu items per recipe (Q69 → plan V2);
  - per-etto items (Q71 → plan V4);
  - menu prices per service period or per room;
  - the founder answering Q68–Q74 differently (variants V1–V6 of the M17 plan).
